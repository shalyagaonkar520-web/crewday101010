Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026 | Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the CrewDay mobile application for Android, available on the Google Play Store, and its related services.

1. Introduction

CrewDay ("we", "our", or "us") provides a platform for discovering, organizing, and attending community events, social meetups, and group activities. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide and operate the application:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and optional profile picture. If you sign in via Google Sign-In, Google securely shares your name, email, and profile avatar with us. We never receive or store your Google password.
  • Profile Details: You may optionally provide a phone number, city, neighborhood/area, and personal interests to help you connect with relevant local events.
  • Event Registrations & Tickets: Records of events you join, ticket tokens, registration timestamps, and attendee check-in status.
  • Payment Information: For paid events, transactions are processed directly by our authorized payment gateway, Razorpay. We collect and retain only transaction metadata (such as Razorpay Order ID, Payment ID, and transaction amount) for order verification and legal compliance. Credit card, debit card, UPI credentials, and bank account numbers are submitted directly to Razorpay over TLS/SSL encryption and never reach or get stored on CrewDay servers.
  • Device & Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver push notifications about event updates, registration confirmations, or reminders if you enable notifications.
  • Analytics: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage data (such as anonymous registration counts) to monitor platform stability and performance.

3. Android Device Permissions

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): The camera permission is strictly optional and requested only when an authorized event organizer uses the in-app QR scanner to verify attendee tickets at event check-in. Regular attendees are never requested for camera access. No photos, videos, or camera recordings are ever saved, stored, or transmitted to our servers.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to synchronize event data, handle authentication, and communicate securely with our cloud backend.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Creating and maintaining your user account.
  • Processing event registrations and issuing ticket QR codes.
  • Allowing event organizers to verify your ticket and manage attendee rosters.
  • Delivering important transactional notifications (e.g., ticket confirmation, schedule changes).
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and ensuring platform safety.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or data brokers. Information is shared only in the following specific contexts:

  • Event Organizers: When you register for an event, the designated organizer of that event can view your name, email address, phone number, and attendance status to safely coordinate the gathering.
  • Fellow Attendees: In your profile settings, you have full control over whether your name and interests appear in the attendee list to other participants.
  • Service Providers: We use reputable infrastructure service providers who act strictly as data processors:
    • Google Firebase (Google Cloud): For secure authentication, database hosting (Cloud Firestore), and push notifications.
    • Razorpay: For secure payment gateway processing.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority to comply with legal obligations.

6. Data Security

All data transmitted between the CrewDay application and our backend systems is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Database access is restricted through fine-grained server-side security rules, ensuring users can only access their own private data and authorized organizer resources.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account at any time directly in the app by navigating to Profile → Settings → Danger zone → Delete account.
  • Web Deletion URL: If you uninstalled the app or prefer a web-based request, visit our public deletion page: https://crewday-2657d.web.app/delete-account.
  • Email Request: You can send an email to shalyagaonkar@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my CrewDay account". We will process your verified request within 7 business days.

What happens upon deletion: Your sign-in credentials, personal name, email, phone number, profile photo, location, and interests are permanently removed from our active database. Upcoming event registrations are cancelled and released. Past attendance is retained only as an anonymous numerical count for organizer record-keeping, and payment records are retained only as required by financial regulations.

8. Children's Privacy

CrewDay is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
